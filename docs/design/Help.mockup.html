Help: the info button and the help sheet

A: the info button sits at the right end of the header, 8 px after the stems pill. It's a 38 px circle in the pill style with an "i" glyph, and it's the same on every screen. Here it's hovered.

B: the sheet, opened on the Play tab. It replaces the screen inside the panel, and the info button is lit. The tabs sit in the title row. Each tab shows four full-width tips that share the height. Solid keycaps are keys; dashed caps (the Drag chips' style, with a mouse glyph) are mouse actions and buttons. The × or the info button closes the sheet, and the screen comes back as it was.

C: the Stems tab.

D: the Loop tab.

E: the Export tab.
