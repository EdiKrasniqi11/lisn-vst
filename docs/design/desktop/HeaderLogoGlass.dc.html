Flag: the plum disc on Midnight

The reversed mark's plum disc (#5E3A4D) is a warm purple, and Midnight is navy (#101A2E). The disc stands out from it by only 1.8:1, and its warm hue pulls against the blue, so the disc reads as a muddy brown coin. On Dusk's mauve (#7B5467) it is 1.5:1 and reads as a darker coin, which is how the reversed mark is meant to look.

The guide's answer for backgrounds the reversed mark doesn't suit: the one-colour mark (one-colour/*-white.svg, the rows below) or the mark on a cream tile (the app icon, right). Both read cleanly on navy. The one-colour mark loses the stem colours, the tile keeps them. Recolouring the disc navy would break the guide's "keep the disc and wave" rule, so it isn't shown.
